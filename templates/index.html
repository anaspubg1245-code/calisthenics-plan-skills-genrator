HOW THE PROGRESSION SYSTEM WORKS

1. Foundation

Build mobility, joint preparation, basic strength and body control before harder variations.

2. Progression

Increase difficulty gradually through easier variations, holds, controlled reps and technique drills.

3. Skill Practice

Practice the selected skill with short, high-quality sets and adequate recovery.

4. Deload & Test

Recovery days reduce fatigue. The final days can be used for a controlled technique check rather than forcing a maximum.
